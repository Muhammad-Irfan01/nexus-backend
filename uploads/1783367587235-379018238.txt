Employee Handbook

Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days annually. Any additional sick leave requires approval from the HR department.

Working Hours

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.

Probation Period

New employees are subject to a probation period of three months.
