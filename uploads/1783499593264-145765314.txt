Nexus CRM Product Guide

Overview

Nexus CRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses.

Features

- Contact Management
- Lead Tracking
- Sales Pipeline Management
- Email Automation
- Reporting Dashboard

Pricing

Starter Plan: $29/month
Professional Plan: $79/month
Enterprise Plan: $199/month

Support

Email support is available for all plans.

Enterprise customers receive dedicated account managers and priority support.

Integrations

Nexus CRM integrates with Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and Microsoft Teams.
